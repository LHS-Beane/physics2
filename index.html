Student Support

These sections help students know what each weekly page should contain and how to use the course site successfully.

How to Use Each Week

Start with the overview, read the notes carefully, work through the examples, complete homework, and use the review section before quizzes or exams.

What to Bring to Class

Bring your notebook, calculator, completed pre-class reading or notes, and any printed workbook pages or digital access to the week’s resource page.

Study Strategy

Do not only reread. Rework examples, sketch diagrams, explain ideas in words, and practice setting up problems before solving them numerically.

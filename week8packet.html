§1–2 Hook & Einstein's Postulates

Classical physics breaks down

Classical velocity addition

Newton/Galileo would say $v_{total}=v_{train}+v_{ball}$.

$60+40=100\text{ mph}$. This works well for ordinary objects.

Now use a flashlight

Classical prediction for a train moving at $0.5c$ with a forward flashlight:

Classically: $c+0.5c=1.5c$. Experimentally: every observer still measures $c=3.00\times10^8\,\text{m/s}$. That contradiction forces a new model of space and time.
Postulate 1 — Principle of Relativity

The laws of physics are the same in all inertial reference frames. A smooth airplane with closed windows is the everyday example: you cannot use a local physics experiment to prove you are moving at constant velocity.

Postulate 2 — Constancy of Light Speed

All observers measure vacuum light speed as $c$, no matter how the source or observer moves. To preserve this, measurements of time and length must change between observers.

§3 Time Dilation

The light clock derivation

Big idea: The photon must still travel at $c$. In the moving frame it travels a longer diagonal path, so the tick takes more time. Moving clocks run slower.
$(c\Delta t)^2=(c\Delta t_0)^2+(v\Delta t)^2$ if using the full up-down tick. If using the half-tick triangle, use $(c\Delta t/2)^2=(c\Delta t_0/2)^2+(v\Delta t/2)^2$. Both produce the same final equation.
$c^2\Delta t^2=c^2\Delta t_0^2+v^2\Delta t^2$ → $\Delta t^2(c^2-v^2)=c^2\Delta t_0^2$ → $\Delta t^2(1-v^2/c^2)=\Delta t_0^2$ → $\Delta t=\dfrac{\Delta t_0}{\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}}=\gamma\Delta t_0$.

Interactive Lorentz factor calculator

$\gamma =$ 1.667

A 1.0 s proper-time tick is measured as 1.667 s by the observer who sees the clock moving.

§4 Reality Check

Muons and GPS prove this is real

Cosmic muons

Muons form about 10 km above Earth. Their rest-frame half-life is $2.2\,\mu s$, but many reach the ground because at $v=0.998c$, $\gamma\approx15.8$.

$\gamma=1/\sqrt{1-0.998^2}\approx15.82$. Lifetime in Earth's frame: $15.82(2.2\,\mu s)\approx34.8\,\mu s$. Distance: $(0.998)(3.0\times10^8)(34.8\times10^{-6})\approx1.04\times10^4\,m$, or about 10.4 km.

GPS correction

SR: slower by about $7\,\mu s/day$. GR: faster by about $45\,\mu s/day$. Net: $+38\,\mu s/day$. Without correction, GPS error grows by roughly 11 km/day.
§5 Length Contraction & Simultaneity

Space changes too

Length contraction

$L=L_0/\gamma=L_0\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}$. $L_0$ is proper/rest length, the longest length. $L$ is contracted length measured when the object moves. Only the direction of motion contracts.

Rocket practice

A rocket has proper length $L_0=500\,m$ and travels at $0.8c$.

$\gamma=1/\sqrt{1-0.8^2}=1/0.6=1.667$. $L=500/1.667\approx300\,m$.

Relativity of simultaneity

Lightning strikes both ends of a moving train simultaneously in the ground frame.

The ground observer sees both flashes arrive together. The train observer moves toward the front flash and away from the rear flash. Since both light signals still travel at $c$, the front flash reaches the train observer first. Events simultaneous in one frame do not have to be simultaneous in another.
§6 Energy & Mass

Mass is energy

Relativistic energy

1 gram of mass

Find the energy stored in $m=0.001\,kg$.

$E=mc^2=(0.001)(3.0\times10^8)^2=9.0\times10^{13}\,J$. A tiny mass contains enormous energy.

Energy-momentum relation

$E^2=(pc)^2+(mc^2)^2$

At rest: $E=mc^2$. Photon: $E=pc=hf$. Low-speed limit: $E\approx mc^2+\frac12mv^2$.
§7–9 General Relativity & Black Holes

Gravity becomes geometry

Newton vs. Einstein

Newton: force. Einstein: curvature of spacetime. Mass tells spacetime how to curve; spacetime tells mass how to move.

Black holes

Not even light can escape. $r_s=2GM/c^2$. The event horizon is the point-of-no-return boundary, not a solid surface.

Gravitational time dilation near a black hole

$\tau/t=\sqrt{1-r_s/r}$

At $2r_s$: $\sqrt{1-1/2}=0.707$. At $4r_s$: $\sqrt{1-1/4}=0.866$. Far away: $1$. At the horizon: $0$ for a distant observer.

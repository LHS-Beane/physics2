Master Equation Reference

These are the equations you should be comfortable recognizing and using by the end of the week.

Day 1 equations

$$F = |q|vB\sin\theta$$
$$r = \frac{mv}{|q|B}$$
$$f = \frac{|q|B}{2\pi m}$$
$$B_{\text{moving charge}} = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{qv\sin\theta}{r^2}$$

Day 2 equations

$$B_{\text{wire}} = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}$$
$$B_{\text{loop center}} = \frac{\mu_0 N I}{2R}$$
$$B_{\text{solenoid}} = \mu_0 n I$$
$$F = ILB\sin\theta \qquad \tau = \mu B\sin\theta$$

Practice Problems with Worked Solutions

Try each one on paper first. Open the solution only after you have committed to an approach.

Before the test, make sure you can do these

  • Use the right-hand rules without guessing.
  • Explain why magnetic force does no work.
  • Find the field of a straight wire and interpret the direction.
  • Connect magnetic force to circular motion and solve for radius or frequency.
  • Explain why a current loop behaves like a magnetic dipole.
  • Describe how a solenoid creates a nearly uniform field inside.

Why Gauss’s Law Is Tricky

Common Student Mistake

Trying to use Gauss’s Law just because the formula is available, even when the symmetry is weak.

Better Question

Ask first: “What symmetry exists?” Then ask: “What Gaussian surface matches it?”

Strong Cases

Point charge, line charge, and infinite plane are the classics because the field behaves predictably.

Teaching Win

Discovery first, equation second. That flip makes the whole topic feel much less brutal.
